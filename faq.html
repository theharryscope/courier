How Can We Help You Today?

Browse answers to frequently asked questions about tracking numbers, pickup bookings, shipping tariffs, delivery timelines, and restricted packages.

You can track your package by entering your tracking number (e.g., SHL202610001) into our Track Shipment Portal. You will receive live milestone updates, current dispatch city, estimated time of arrival, and driver contact details when out for delivery.
To request a pickup, visit our Book Delivery Page, fill in your pickup address, recipient details, and package weight, and select your preferred pickup time. A courier rider will be assigned immediately.
  • Same-Day Express (Intra-City): Delivered within 2–6 hours in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.
  • Inter-State Standard: Delivered within 24 to 48 hours nationwide.
  • International Express Freight: 3 to 5 business days via air freight partners.
