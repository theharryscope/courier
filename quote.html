Volumetric Weight vs. Actual Weight

Logistics companies calculate charges based on space taken up as well as actual weight. For example, a large box of pillows weighs very little but takes up significant cargo space on our airplanes and haulage trucks.

IATA Volumetric Formula:

[ Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm) ] / 5000

* Billable Weight = MAX ( Actual Weight, Volumetric Weight )
