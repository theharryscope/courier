Our Origin & Journey

Reinventing Freight & Urban Dispatch in Africa's Largest Economy

Founded in 2021 in Sabo Yaba, Lagos—Nigeria’s bustling technology hub—SwiftHaul Logistics began with a clear mission: to resolve the operational friction, package delays, and lack of transparency plaguing traditional parcel dispatch.

What started with a modest fleet of ten urban motorcycle dispatchers has evolved into a multimodal logistics power. Today, we operate primary distribution hubs across Lagos (Ikeja, Lekki, Apapa), Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan, and Enugu, while integrating air cargo lines for international exports to over 200 countries.
